# Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Definition

The Minimum Viable Product for this e-commerce platform consists of **Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3**.

## Scope of MVP
The MVP delivers the core end-to-end e-commerce flow:
1. **Foundation & Authentication (Phase 1)**: Users can log in securely via Google OAuth.
2. **Product Catalog & Search (Phase 2)**: Users can browse the storefront, search with typo-tolerance, and view product details.
3. **Wishlist, Cart & Checkout (Phase 3)**: Users can add items to a cart or wishlist and complete a purchase securely.

## Exclusions from MVP
The following features are slated for post-MVP releases:
- Order history tracking and offline handling (Phase 4)
- Personalized algorithmic recommendations (Phase 5)
- Full Admin Panel for inventory and order management (Phase 6)
- Analytics dashboards and massive scale testing (Phase 7)
